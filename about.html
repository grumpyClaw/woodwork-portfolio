About

Traditional craftsmanship meets contemporary Australian design

The Craftsman

Mark Gibson is a Queensland-based woodworker creating handcrafted timber furniture for homes across Australia. Each piece is made to order, combining traditional joinery techniques with clean, contemporary lines.

Philosophy

Good furniture should last generations. That's why every piece uses solid Australian hardwoods, time-tested joinery methods, and natural finishes that protect without hiding the wood's character.

Process

From selecting boards with the right grain to hand-fitting joints and applying the final oil finish, each step is done with attention to detail. No shortcuts, no mass production - just honest craftsmanship.

Materials & Methods

Timbers

  • Queensland Walnut
  • Oak
  • Pine
  • Recycled hardwoods

Joinery

  • Mortise and tenon
  • Dovetails
  • Frame-and-panel
  • Wedged through-tenons

Finishes

  • Danish oil
  • Beeswax
  • Clear lacquer
  • Natural stains
